About the Farm

Rooted in Cassava, Built on Integrity

B. D. Oboh Integrity Farm was established in 2021 in Otefe, Oghara, Ethiope West, Delta State. We grow cassava across a 150-acre farm and process it into garri, cassava flour, fufu and white amala in commercial quantities for local and international markets. We also sell fresh cassava tubers.

Our philosophy is simple: grow good food honestly, process it carefully, and treat the land and the people around us with respect. Cassava is one of Nigeria's most important food crops, and we are committed to adding value to it right here at home.

  • Our Vision

    To be a trusted Nigerian name in cassava farming and processing, known for quality, honesty and reliability.

  • Our Mission

    To grow and process wholesome cassava products, create dependable jobs and support the community around us.

Management

Hon. Benjamin D. Oboh

CEO / Managing Director

Hon. Oboh leads the farm's operations and direction, overseeing cassava production, processing and supply. Under his leadership, the farm focuses on agriculture, product quality and steady growth as a professionally managed agribusiness.

Our commitment to Nigerian agriculture: add value to what we grow, keep processing standards high, and keep opportunity close to home.
